# To Hold a Mountain

Static website, served by Render from `public/` (no build step).

- `public/favicon.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` — the icon: mountain ridges in the evening haze
- `tools/cloud_gaps.py` — opens soft gaps in the cloud bank (from `tools/clouds-solid.webp`), where a blue sky behind it shows
- `public/laurels.webp` — festival laurels at the top of the homepage, nine in one strip, each sized to the same visual weight, built from the trimmed originals in `tools/laurels/` (one row beside the menu button; on phones the same strip in one row above the logo, drifting right to left for ever and draggable (main.js))
- `public/index.html`, `style.css`, `main.js` — homepage: logo over the sky photo (`sky.webp`), the storm clouds (`clouds.webp`, a seamless sideways tile drifting steadily with the wind; the sky photo behind the logo moves up at a third of the scrolling; the logo and its button are masked with `clouds-mask.webp` so they go behind the bank (hidden even where it is only haze) instead of showing through; nearer layers move and drift faster the closer they are: puffs between the bank and the texts (`clouds-mid.webp`) and see-through wisps in front of them (`clouds-veil.webp`, well blurred), all made by `tools/cloud_depth.py`; the wisps (already up in the sky over the logo when the page opens, sparse there) cast shadows of their own shape and edge (soft or crisp) 0.2 of a screen straight below themselves, as from a high sun (none behind a wisp; baked into `clouds-shade.webp`; the wisps are one long strip, few of them, fading away as the video comes up, with clear sky around the logo as the page opens and a group above the last quote, for its shadows), on the letters of the logo, the texts and the press logos only (a darkened copy of the logo masked with `clouds-shade.webp`; the texts' colour is a background clipped to the letters with those shapes over it), following their drift; the logline and quotes ride up into the centre and stay, the last (Variety) going behind the cumulus near the bank's bottom as it rises (masked with `clouds-below-mask.webp`); arriving over the top third of the video and staying there with it, blurring with it and dissolving as the synopsis comes into focus, made from the storm photo by `tools/clouds.py`: a top edge shaped by the cloud forms, softened toward the film's overcast sky lower down, the thick middle extended, the bottom faded) scroll up over it carrying the logline and two press quotes (Screen Daily, then Variety; logos `press-*.png`), each cross-fading into the next and fade onto the film clip (`bg2-light.mp4`, 1280 wide and under 1 MB, is what the loading screen waits for; the full-quality `bg2.mp4`, 12 s, 1920×1072, H.264, no audio, then loads behind it and fades in over it, in step; `bg2-poster.webp`, its first frame, shows until it plays), which plays and loops on its own while on screen (on phones it starts 2 s in the first time, then loops whole; `bg2-poster-mobile.webp` is that frame); scrolling on blurs it (not fully; no zoom); as it blurs, the poster (`poster.webp`), the synopsis and the About the film and trailer buttons come into focus in place, over the blurred video, still playing (on phones stacked: poster full width, heading, synopsis, buttons, footer, moved up by main.js over the pinned video as you scroll, from the moment it starts to show), and the page ends there. A loading screen (the light sky colour #939ca2, a small dark blue bar and percentage) holds the page until fonts, images and the clip are loaded; then the logo comes into focus
- `public/gate.js` — password screen on every page (remembered in the browser once entered). Keeps casual visitors out only: the files themselves stay reachable. To change the password, put its hash (see `hash()` in the file) in `HASH`
- `public/trailer.js` — "Watch the trailer" buttons (under the logo and beside About the film) open the YouTube trailer in a player over the darkened page
- `public/site.css`, `menu.js`, `menu.html` — shared styles and the menu (Home, About the film, Trailer (opens the player), Screenings, Press, Soundscape, Contact (Community hidden for now, its page kept); a pill at the top right: three lines, then MENU; it opens the menu as a page of its own, `menu.html`, in its own darker olive, so iPhone Safari's bands match it; the X goes back to the page you came from, where you were; pages cross-fade where browsers can): listed at the top of `menu.js`, the page you came from in italics, and the footer on every page (credits, designer, Instagram, Facebook, IMDb, TMDB; on the homepage at the bottom of the synopsis screen)
- `public/about.html`, `about.css` — directors' statement, the directors' photo (`directors.jpg`), their bios, credits and additional information
- `public/contact.html` — contacts (US theatrical: Submarine Deluxe; everything else), laid out like the credits (about.css)
- `public/press.html`, `press.css`, `press.js`, `press/` — what the critics wrote: the outlet's logo (white, `press/*.webp`, each sized in the page for about the same visual weight), stars where given, the quote in italics, the critic; three columns (two on tablets, one on phones), each fading up as it comes into view
- `public/screenings.html`, `screenings.css`, `sheet.js` — the Screenings & Awards table (Date, Location, Selection), filled when the page opens from a Google Sheet published to the web as CSV (its address and tab id at the top of `sheet.js`); edits show within a few minutes (Google's servers can send an old copy for a while after an edit); the headings are in the page, grey skeleton bars stand in for the rows until the Sheet has been read, then the rows fade in
- `public/community.html`, `community.css`, `community.js` — placeholder text and a looping stack of prints (click on computers; tap or swipe either way on phones) (`gallery/01.jpg` … `21.jpg`, the first one a plain #78879d until it loads, then fading in; to add photos, add the next numbers and raise `data-count` in the page)
- `public/soundtrack.html`, `soundtrack.css`, `soundtrack.js` — one-track player ("The Last Ones (Outro)", Drasko Adzic, `soundtrack.mp3`) with a soundwave, over `bg3.mp4` (grass in the wind, a seamless 3.2 s loop; a plain #615738 until it plays, then it fades in)

New track: replace `public/soundtrack.mp3`, then regenerate the soundwave:

```bash
ffmpeg -v error -i public/soundtrack.mp3 -ac 1 -ar 8000 -f s16le - | python3 -c "import sys,array,json; a=array.array('h',sys.stdin.buffer.read()); n=len(a)//200; p=[max(abs(x) for x in a[i*n:(i+1)*n]) for i in range(200)]; m=max(p); json.dump([round(x/m,3) for x in p],open('public/soundtrack-peaks.json','w'))"
```

Run locally:

```bash
npx serve public
```

Every push to `main` deploys automatically.
